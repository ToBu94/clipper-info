Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who uses this app

highlight-clipper is a private tool used only by its operator to upload video clips to the operator's own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

What data is accessed

The app requests two Google permissions:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – to upload videos created by the tool to the operator's own channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly – to read aggregated statistics of the operator's own videos (views, average watch percentage, traffic sources), so the tool can learn which clips work.

The app does not read, collect or analyse any other Google account data and no data of viewers or other channels.

Storage

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted on the operator's own computer. They are not sent to any third party. No personal data of viewers or other people is collected or stored.

Sharing

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are then deleted from the operator's computer.

Questions can be raised via the issue tracker of this page.
